# Project portfolio — user guide

## What it is

A portal where the company keeps all its projects in one place:
- every project has a **card**: participants, timeline, budget, health, change history, comments, status reports and risks;
- the PM regularly submits a **status report** — it is the only way to change the project's key indicators (status, health, % complete, dates, costs);
- project **risks and issues** are scored as "probability × impact";
- the **home page** shows the portfolio: how many projects are in which state, the trend, projects at risk and decision requests.

Everyone sees only the projects where they or their direct reports have a role.

## How to open

- **Computer:** open `https://smarthrua.sharepoint.com/sites/pmo-test` in a browser and sign in with your Microsoft 365 work account.
- **Phone and tablet (iPhone, iPad, Android):** the same address in Safari or Chrome. To open it like an app: in Safari — Share → "Add to Home Screen", in Chrome — menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen".
- **The SharePoint mobile app** may not show the portal fully — use the browser in that case.

This guide is always available under the **?** button at the top of the portal.

## Who sees what and can do what

| Who | Sees | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Project PM | own project | edit the card, submit status reports, manage risks, comment |
| The PM's managers (org chart) | projects of their reports | view and comment |
| Owner, stakeholders and their managers | projects where they or their reports have a role | view and comment |
| PMO | all projects | create new projects, view and comment |
| Other employees | an empty portal | — |

An archived project is view-only for everyone. If the portal is empty for you, you have no role in any project.

## Home

- **Portfolio by health** — a ring: how many active projects are green, yellow, red and not rated yet.
- **Portfolio health over time** — how health changed over the last 12 weeks (a snapshot every two weeks).
- **Projects at risk**, **No recent status report** (over 14 days), **Management decisions needed**, **Open risks** — click "See all" to open the full list.

Everything is counted only over the projects you can see.

## Projects

- **Tiles** or **List** — the switch at the top. Tiles are handier on a phone.
- **Views:** Усі проєкти (all), Стратегічні (strategic), Проблемні (at risk), Мої проєкти (where I am PM, owner or stakeholder), Немає свіжого звіту (no recent report). View names are in Ukrainian in every language.
- **In the list:** click a column header to sort; the funnel ▽ filters by value; the gear ⚙ chooses columns. New records are on top.
- **Colours:** a red completion date means the deadline has passed and the project is still open. Report light: green — up to 8 days, yellow — 8–14, red — over 14.

## Project card

Click a project and its card opens on the right (full screen on a phone):
- at the top — type, health, status, priority, the Loop link; **Add status report** and **Edit** buttons (PM only);
- **Stakeholders**, **Timeline** (with the forecast deviation), **Budget and progress**;
- **Change history** — who changed what and when: "old → new", the reason;
- **Comments** — the feed and "Add comment";
- **Health history** — ratings from the latest reports;
- **Status reports**, **Risks and issues**;
- **Card access** — who sees the project and with what rights.

You can share a link to the card: the browser address points exactly to it.

## Status report (for the PM)

1. In the card — **Add status report** (or the "Status reports" tab → "New status report").
2. Rate **schedule, budget and resources**: green, yellow, red. **Overall health** is calculated automatically — the worst of the three.
3. **Key indicators** are prefilled with the current values. Change only what has changed: status, % complete, type, dates, actual costs.
4. If you changed the status, type or a date, fill in **Reason for changing indicators** (required, goes to the history).
5. A **one-line summary** (required), what was done, the plan, issues; tick **Management decision needed** if one is needed and describe it.
6. **Save.** The card updates immediately.

The **"Завершено" (Completed)** status sends the project to the archive — after that it is view-only.

## Risks and issues

- Add one from the card ("Add risk", PM only) or on the "Risks and issues" tab.
- **Probability** and **impact** are 1 to 5; the **score** = probability × impact: 15–25 — high (red), 8–14 — medium (yellow), 1–7 — low (green).
- Set the risk owner, status (Відкрито — open, В роботі — in progress, Закрито — closed), due date and mitigation. An overdue open risk is shown in red.

## Comments

Anyone who sees the project can comment: in the card — "Comments" → "Add comment".

## New project and editing

- A **new project** is created by the PMO: the name and the PM are required; if no code is given, one is assigned automatically (PRJ-001, PRJ-002…).
- Only the PM can **edit** the card: name, code, department, Loop, priority, participants, budget, description. Status, health, dates and costs change only through a status report.
- After participants change, access rights are recalculated automatically.

## Archive

The "Archive" tab lists completed projects with their archive date. Cards are view-only.

## Language and theme

Top right: **UA / EN / RU** and the light ☀ / dark ☾ theme. The choice is remembered in this browser. Values (statuses, health) and view names stay in Ukrainian in every language.

## When changes appear

Anything you save in the forms appears on screen immediately. Access rights, the change history and "Card access" update automatically within ~15 minutes.

## Feedback

The **Feedback** button at the top: describe what is wrong or what could be better and add up to 5 screenshots:
- on a computer — take a screenshot and paste it into the form: **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Cmd+V** (Mac); or use "Add screenshot";
- on a phone or tablet — "Add screenshot" → gallery or camera.

The screen you opened the feedback from and your device are added automatically. Your feedback is visible to you and the PMO.

## Tasks for the focus group

Do what applies to your role and after each task leave **feedback**: what was unclear, inconvenient or did not work.

1. Open the portal on a computer and on a phone. Is it clear from the home page what is happening with the portfolio?
2. Find your projects (the "Мої проєкти" view). Are all your projects there, and nothing extra?
3. Open a project card: participants, timeline, change history, access. What is missing?
4. **PM:** submit a status report: rate schedule, budget and resources, change the completion date (with a reason).
5. **PM:** add a risk, rate probability and impact, set the owner and due date.
6. **Everyone:** add a comment to a project where you are the owner or a stakeholder.
7. **Managers:** make sure you see your reports' projects but cannot change them.
8. In the projects list try sorting, filters and choosing columns.
9. Switch the language and theme.
10. Send feedback with a screenshot from your phone.
